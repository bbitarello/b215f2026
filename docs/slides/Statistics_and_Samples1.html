What is statistics?

Statistics deals with UNCERTAINTY

  • Statistics are a quantitative technology for empirical science.
  • A logic and methodology for the measurement of uncertainty and for an examination of that uncertainty.
  • The key word here is uncertainty. Statistics becomes necessary when observations are variable.

Challenge 2

  • Samples deviate from the populations they were sampled from by

  1. luck/chance (sampling error) or

  2. unrepresentative sampling (sampling bias)

  • Solution:

    • Estimate the values of important parameters

    • Have measures of uncertainty for estimates

    • Test hypotheses about those parameters

In conclusion: goals of statistics

  1. Estimate the values of important parameters

  2. Have measures of uncertainty for estimates

  3. Test hypotheses about those parameters

POPULATIONS & SAMPLES

STATISTICS & PARAMETERS

Populations & Samples

In Statistics, a population is the entire collection of individual units that a researcher is interested in. E.g. all women born in the US between 1990 and 200; all polar bears currently living in zoos; users of a certain social network in a certain age group; etc

A sample is a subset of these units that we collect and measure.

Statistics & Parameters (1/3)

A parameter is some property of the world, i.e., the “truth”

Decorative image. Poster from the show “The X-files”

Statistics & Parameters (2/3)

  • Because we can’t sample an entire population, we usually don’t know parameters. Parameters describe Populations

  • But we can get a good sense of the parameters from estimates we make from Samples.

  • Estimates (statistics) approximate parameters as inferred from Samples

Statistics & Parameters (3/3)

  • Parameters are constants. This means that they are fixed. Parameters represent the world. Parameters are the truth.

  • Estimates are “random variables”. This means estimates change from one random sample to the next from the same population.

A population of starfish

  • Parameters describe populations
  • E.g. proportion of pink starfish among all starfish of a given species in a certain location

A sample of starfish

  • Estimates (statistics) approximate parameters as inferred from samples
  • We estimate the proportion of pink as inferred from this sample and extrapolate to the population as an approximation.
